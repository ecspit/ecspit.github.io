Projects & Research

Here are a collection of personal and professional projects I have worked on.

July 2026 - August 2026 (2 months)

Non-Newtonian Rheology in an Elastic Vessel

Supervised by Dr Marianna Pepona, derived an analytical ∆p-Q relationship for power-law fluids, implemented the model using the Lattice Boltzmann Method in C++, and validated numerical results against analytical predictions. Extended the study to complex regimes without analytical solutions.

July 2025 - August 2025 (2 months)

STEP Solution Database

The STEP is the mathematics entrance exam used by the University of Cambridge et al as a part of their mathematics or maths-related courses' entry requirements. I have produced written solutions and discussions to various questions displayed on a website. Each document includes hints, a complete written solution and a discussion about the question.
